# Memory Lane Songbook

Big sing-along lyrics for the TV when David performs in memory care. No sound: David plays live.

**Open it:** https://davyrockett.github.io/memory-lane/ (in the TV's web browser; use Full screen on the welcome page).

## At the TV (keyboard)
- **Song list:** arrows to move, **Enter** to open, **Tab** to switch groups. Songs show in a mixed-up order that changes each time
  (so the A songs don't always come first); songs already sung today go to the end. **A–Z** shows them alphabetically. The ⌂ button (or **Home** / **Esc**) goes back to the welcome screen.
- **Title card:** **F** another fun fact, **L** "Listen for…", **Enter** to start, **Esc** back. Everything is clickable too.
- **Lyrics:** one verse or chorus at a time, in huge text. Press to go on: **Space**, **→**, **Page Down** or **Enter**
  (a foot pedal or presenter clicker works too). **←** or **Page Up** goes back, **Esc** goes back to the songs. The faded
  line at the bottom shows what's next. After the last section, the questions come up.
  The small bar at the bottom (move the mouse to bring it up): Songs · Restart · Back / Next · Size · Colors · Questions.
  Every song opens with two sections per screen. **Aa Bigger** shows one section at a time for that song;
  **Aa Smaller** goes back to two.
  Prefer the old autoscroll? Settings → "Moving through the words" → Scroll by itself.
- **After the song:** questions to talk about appear by themselves. **→** next question, **Enter** next song,
  or "Sing it again". Songs sung today get a small ✓ on the song list.
- **C** switches colors anytime (cream, white on black, yellow on blue).

## Changing songs
Settings (⚙ on the song list) → **Edit songs**. Add a song, fix lyrics, facts, "Listen for…", questions, groups and the scroll time.
In lyrics, leave a blank line between verses and put `[Chorus]` on its own line at the top of a chorus.
To save changes for every device, paste your GitHub access key in Settings (it needs memory-lane-data with Contents "Read and write").

## Test on the Mac
Double-click **Start Memory Lane.command**.

Works on iPad and iPhone too: tap or swipe left for the next section, swipe right to go back.

## TV link (laptop controls the TV, no cable)
1. On the TV's web browser, open the app and press **📺 Use as TV screen** on the welcome page.
2. On your laptop (or phone / iPad), open the app and press **🎛 Control a TV**.
3. That's it: whatever you do on the laptop shows on the TV within a moment, and the laptop shows the same screen.
   The badge in the top-left corner says **TV linked** when it's working. Both devices remember their job next time.

There's no code to type. Pressing keys on the TV still works too. If the TV loses the connection,
it keeps showing the words, with a small red dot in the corner, and reconnects by itself.

## Laying out a song's slides
While a song is showing, click **✎ Edit** in the bottom bar. You get a slideshow view: all the song's slides down the
left, and the current slide big on the right, at the size it'll be on the TV.
- Type as usual. **Return** starts a new line; **Shift+Return** starts a new slide from the cursor.
- **Backspace** at the very start of a slide joins it onto the slide before. A blank line makes a gap between verses.
- Copy and paste work between slides. **⧉ Repeat slide** adds a copy (e.g. a chorus) that you can drag into place.
- **♪ Chorus** marks the verse under the cursor as a chorus (it shows in the chorus colour).
- Drag slides in the list to reorder them. **↶ Undo** undoes slide changes. **Reset to automatic** goes back to the app's own layout.
- Changes save as you go. Click **Done** (or press Esc) to go back to singing on that slide.
